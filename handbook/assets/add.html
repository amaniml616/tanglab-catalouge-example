Add an item

Take a photo, let AI fill in the form, check every field against the item, and save. The item and its photo are committed to the inventory repository and appear on this page after the site rebuilds (about a minute or two).

Keys and repository (set once per browser)

Keys are stored only in this browser. They are sent only to api.github.com and api.anthropic.com, never to this website. Do not use this on a shared computer.

Get close so the item and any label fill the frame. The photo is resized to about 460 px before it is saved.
